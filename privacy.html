Abe iPhone privacy policy · Effective September 14, 2026

Abe is an offline anatomy and workout planner. It does not send your profile or training records to a Abe server.

What the app stores

Abe stores the training profile you enter, equipment and schedule preferences, workout history, logged sets and weights, readiness adjustments, and an unfinished workout on your iPhone. It uses this information to generate workout recommendations and estimated fatigue. The current release has a local profile, not an online account.

No tracking or remote coaching service

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data. The planner runs on your device; your information is not sent to a remote AI service. Abe does not request access to Apple Health, your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Backups, imports, and deletion

Abe maintains a device-local recovery snapshot. Its training-data folders are excluded from automatic device backup. You can export a JSON backup through the iPhone share sheet and choose where to save or send it. A cloud storage provider you choose may then hold that exported file under its own policy. Importing a backup is optional and displays a review before merging records.

To delete the app’s local profile and records, open Account → iPhone settings & privacy → Delete profile and all local data, then confirm. Copies you previously exported are not deleted by the app. Remove those from their saved locations separately.

Notifications and external links

Optional rest alerts use the iPhone notification system with your permission. They contain a generic rest-complete message and no workout or health details. Exercise images and anatomy assets are bundled. Research, licensing, and support links open external services only when you choose them; those services have their own privacy policies.

Contacting support

If you email ikejdavis@gmail.com, your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach are available to the support recipient and email provider. They are used to respond to your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to any records that must be retained by law. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your question.

This support website

This site does not receive training data from the app and does not use app analytics or advertising cookies. Its hosting provider processes ordinary web requests, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. No workout records are embedded in the website.

Training estimates and audience

Abe is intended for adults planning general fitness training. Fatigue, readiness, muscle emphasis, and suggested weights are adjustable estimates, not measurements, diagnoses, or guaranteed results. The app is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes and questions

This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change. Questions about privacy or support can be sent to ikejdavis@gmail.com.
