Support for Abe, the anatomy and workout planner for iPhone.

Contact Abe support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please don’t email passwords, verification codes, or a full workout backup unless you choose to share it for support.

How does readiness work?

Abe estimates fatigue from the workouts you log, the muscles involved, and the time since training. Readiness starts at 100% when there is no logged fatigue. Tap a muscle on Body to adjust the estimate if it doesn’t match how you feel. These are planning estimates; Abe does not measure muscle damage or recovery.

How do I change my workout?

Open Workout for a complete recommendation based on your local profile and logged training. Tap the workout type, duration, or equipment to change them. Begin workout opens set tracking and the rest timer. Suggested starting weights can be edited after warming up.

How do I save or move my data?

Your profile, history, readiness adjustments, and current workout stay on the device. In Account, open iPhone settings & privacy, then Export backup to Files. On another iPhone, choose Import backup and review the records before merging. Automatic cloud sync is not included.

Can I import my earlier web version backup?

Yes. Export a JSON backup from the web app’s Account / Data menu, save it to Files, then use Import backup in the iPhone app. Existing records are kept and duplicates are skipped. The two apps do not sync automatically.

How do I remove an unfinished workout?

Open the workout and choose Discard workout. It removes the unfinished session without recording it as completed. Completed workout history stays in Account.

How do I delete everything?

In Account, open iPhone settings & privacy and choose Delete profile and all local data. Confirm deletion to remove the profile, history, readiness adjustments, and unfinished workout from Abe. Exported files remain wherever you saved them; remove those separately if needed.

Why didn’t my rest alert appear?

Rest-timer alerts are optional. Enable them in Account → iPhone settings & privacy, and allow notifications in iPhone Settings. While Abe is open, the in-app countdown shows when rest ends. Focus settings and notification permissions can affect alerts.
